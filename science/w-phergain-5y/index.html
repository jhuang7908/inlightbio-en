PHERGain 5-year data: PET-guided chemotherapy de-escalation in HER2-positive early breast cancer

In the randomized phase 2 PHERGain trial (376 patients), patients in group B received trastuzumab and pertuzumab with PET assessment after two cycles; PET responders with pCR skipped chemotherapy. 5-year iDFS in group B was 89.5% (95% CI 85.7–93.4) at a median follow-up of 5.6 years, with exploratory ctDNA analyses.

By Jing Huang, PhD · Generated: Nov 1, 2026

Key data card

  • Study type: Randomized phase 2
  • Sample size n: 376
  • Group A: Docetaxel, carboplatin, trastuzumab and pertuzumab
  • Group B: Trastuzumab + pertuzumab ± endocrine therapy; PET after 2 cycles
  • Follow-up: Median 5.6 years (cutoff 4 Nov 2024)
  • Group B 5-year iDFS: 89.5% (95% CI 85.7–93.4)
  • Group A 5-year iDFS: 96.7% (95% CI 92.2–100), surgery population n=63
  • 5-year OS: 98.4% (A) and 98.1% (B)
  • ctDNA: Detectable ctDNA: 5-year iDFS 86.7% vs 97.1% (HR 3.1, 95% CI 1.0–9.8)
  • Evidence level: Randomized phase 2; ctDNA analyses exploratory
  • Mechanism: early PET response identifies tumors sensitive to dual HER2 blockade alone.

Background and open questions

Most HER2-positive early breast cancers are treated with chemotherapy plus HER2-targeted antibodies. PHERGain tested whether early PET response can identify patients who can omit chemotherapy.

Study design

Patients were randomized to group A (docetaxel, carboplatin, trastuzumab, pertuzumab) or group B (trastuzumab and pertuzumab ± endocrine therapy) with PET after two cycles. PET responders in group B who achieved pCR at surgery did not receive chemotherapy. ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT03161353.

Key results

  • Group B 5-year iDFS was 89.5% (95% CI 85.7–93.4) at a median follow-up of 5.6 years.
  • Group A 5-year iDFS was 96.7% (95% CI 92.2–100) in its 63-patient surgery population.
  • 5-year OS was 98.4% in group A and 98.1% in group B.
  • Detectable ctDNA was associated with worse 5-year iDFS (86.7% vs 97.1%; HR 3.1, 95% CI 1.0–9.8; P = 0.021).

Mechanistic interpretation

PET response after two cycles may help pick patients for whom dual HER2 blockade alone is enough. ctDNA could add risk information but needs validation.

Limitations

  • Phase 2 trial; the 5-year analysis is an extension phase that needed re-consent.
  • ctDNA analyses are exploratory with wide confidence intervals.
