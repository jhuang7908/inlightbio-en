AstraZeneca and CSPC partner on kidney-disease siRNA

Illustration (decorative, not data).
Headline terms

~$1.77B total

$30M upfront + $540M development milestones + $1.2B sales milestones

Source

Fierce Biotech (2026-07-03, citing CSPC's July 2 announcement)

Summary

The two companies will jointly discover and develop small-interfering-RNA drugs against two undisclosed kidney-disease targets, using CSPC's siRNA discovery platform and delivery tools that can carry drugs to organs beyond the liver, plus CSPC's AI molecular-design models and automated high-throughput screening. AstraZeneca may choose global or ex-China exclusive rights; CSPC retains China rights to one of the assets. siRNA has so far been largely limited to liver targets (GalNAc conjugation), and kidney delivery is widely seen as the industry's next hurdle. With only $30 million up front, this is an early discovery deal whose value rests mainly on later stages.
