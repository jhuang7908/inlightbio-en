Summary

TQC3721, a Class 1 innovative drug from Chia Tai Tianqing, is an inhaled suspension that targets both PDE3 and PDE4. It aims to relax airway smooth muscle and inhibit inflammatory-cell pathways at the same time, addressing the limited anti-inflammatory effect of existing LAMA/LABA therapy and the limited benefit of ICS in patients with low eosinophil counts. The pivotal registrational Phase 3 PACER-III has completed enrollment of all participants, a step closer to the primary endpoint readout. The company is also developing nebulized and dry-powder formulations for different patients and settings. Efficacy, safety and differentiation from current maintenance therapy still depend on the full data after unblinding, and completed enrollment should not be read as a sign that approval is near.
