MicuRx licenses MRX-23 ADC and grants STOPIN platform options to ClearideBio

AI-generated illustration.
Headline terms

$1M upfront

$1M non-refundable upfront; MRX-23 milestones capped at a nominal $223M; options on up to 6 STOPIN programs at up to $125M each ($750M if all are exercised); plus sublicensing revenue share or sales royalties

Source

Fineline Cube (2026-10-10), reporting MicuRx's SSE announcement

Summary

MRX-23 is an antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) candidate from MicuRx; its target and indication were not disclosed. ClearideBio Therapeutics AG (Switzerland) takes exclusive worldwide rights to develop, manufacture and commercialize it across indications. Separately, ClearideBio receives options on up to six targets or target combinations from MicuRx's STOPIN ADC platform, built around a novel camptothecin payload; platform rights vest only after an option is exercised and an exercise fee is paid, while MicuRx keeps the platform's core intellectual property. Headline milestone caps are theoretical maxima, not a valuation or expected revenue, and everything beyond the non-refundable upfront depends on options and milestones being reached. With $1 million paid up front against up to $973 million in headline milestones, this is an option-heavy bet on an unproven payload platform by a Swiss licensee, not a conventional China out-licensing payday.

Milestone payments are contingent and may never be paid.
