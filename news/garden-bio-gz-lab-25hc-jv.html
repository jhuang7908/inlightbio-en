Garden Bio and Guangzhou National Laboratory form a joint venture for 25-hydroxycholesterol broad-spectrum antivirals

AI-generated illustration.
Headline terms

Undisclosed

New joint venture run by Garden Biopharma with at least 51%; the rest held by an entity the laboratory designates; phased capital contributions and transfer of IP; no amounts disclosed

Source

VCBeat Health (2026-10-07); Longbridge/Zhitong English flash (2026-10-07)

Summary

25-hydroxycholesterol is a natural oxysterol the partners aim to develop, with prodrugs, as a broad-spectrum antiviral. The program is early-stage: English reports describe a new-drug R&D project entering innovative development, without disclosed clinical data. The joint venture is meant to take the candidates through clinical development, registration, manufacturing and commercialization, with Garden Biopharma holding at least 51% and leading day-to-day operations. Garden Biopharma, best known as a vitamin D3 and cholesterol-series manufacturer, frames the deal as its entry into innovative drugs, while Guangzhou National Laboratory contributes the research collaboration. It is a state-laboratory NewCo with a manufacturer in the controlling seat, and whether an oxysterol becomes an approved antiviral has yet to be tested in people.

Milestone payments are contingent and may never be paid.
