CSPC updates Phase 1 data for SYS6002 in advanced cervical cancer; 2.4 mg/kg regimen moves into Phase 3

Source

CSPC announcement, HKEXnews (2026-10-08)

Summary

The voluntary HKEX announcement gives dose, patient-number and response and survival readouts that can be checked item by item. A nationwide multicenter Phase 1 enrolled 82 patients with previously treated advanced cervical cancer; the confirmed ORR was 35.9% with 2.4 mg/kg every 2 weeks and 28.6% with 3.6 mg/kg every 3 weeks, at a median follow-up of 16.1 months. Safety was generally manageable: ocular adverse events were the most common, but none was grade 3 or higher and none led to discontinuation; the rate of interstitial lung disease/non-infectious pneumonitis was 2.4%. The sponsor chose the 2.4 mg/kg every-2-weeks regimen for Phase 3 (NCT07230626), indicating that this dose offers the preferred balance of efficacy and risk; it still needs to be confirmed in further studies.
