CSPC updates Phase 1 data for SYS6002 in advanced cervical cancer; 2.4 mg/kg regimen moves into Phase 3

Phase 1, 82 patients; confirmed ORR 35.9% (2.4 mg/kg Q2W) and 28.6% (3.6 mg/kg Q3W); median DoR 8.2 and 10.3 months; median PFS 4.1 and 4.3 months; median OS 15.1 and 11.5 months; 2.4 mg/kg Q2W now in Phase 3 (NCT07230626)

Source

CSPC HKEX English announcement (2026-10-08); ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07230626

Summary

SYS6002 is a Nectin-4 antibody-drug conjugate that delivers the mitotic inhibitor MMAE. It uses enzyme-catalysed site-specific conjugation and a stable linker intended to concentrate toxin in the tumour while limiting systemic exposure. In a nationwide multicentre Phase 1 in China, 82 patients with previously treated advanced cervical cancer received 2.4 mg/kg, every 2 weeks (39 patients) or 3.6 mg/kg every 3 weeks (43 patients); at the 8 May 2026 cutoff (median follow-up 16.1 months), both regimens showed preliminary activity — confirmed ORR, median DoR, median PFS and median OS are in the side box, with PFS and OS reported in months. Treatment-related adverse events were mostly grade 1–2: ocular events were most common (none grade ≥3; no discontinuations for them); interstitial lung disease or non-infectious pneumonitis 2.4%; rash 20.7% (grade ≥3, 1.2%); peripheral neuropathy 12.2% (all grade 1–2); no treatment-related deaths. Data were a rapid oral at IGCS 2026. The 2.4 mg/kg Q2W regimen has entered Phase 3 (NCT07230626) after immunotherapy, which ClinicalTrials.gov lists as an estimated 412 patients randomized to SYS6002 versus investigator's-choice chemotherapy with overall survival as the primary endpoint. These are single-arm Phase 1 data; that head-to-head Phase 3 will show whether a response rate near 36% turns into a survival benefit.
