NewCo Services

China now originates a large share of the world's licensable clinical assets. A NewCo turns one of those assets into a US company: investors fund it, an experienced US team runs global development, and the Chinese originator keeps Greater China rights plus cash, equity, milestones and royalties. We work on both sides of that transaction: sourcing and packaging China assets, and connecting them with the investors, founders and pharma partners who build around them.

How the NewCo model works

A Chinese company licenses ex-Greater China rights to a newly formed company (usually a Delaware corporation). Investors fund the NewCo, typically a Series A sized to reach a clinical inflection point, and recruit a US leadership team. The licensor is paid an upfront amount (cash and/or NewCo equity), development, regulatory and sales milestones, and royalties, and keeps its home-market rights. Value is realized when the NewCo raises later rounds, lists, or is acquired by a pharma company: Aiolos Bio (Hengrui asset) was acquired by GSK, and Ouro Medicines (Keymed asset) by Gilead; see the deal table.

Typical trade-offs: a licensor gives up near-term cash compared with a big-pharma license in exchange for equity upside and a dedicated global team; investors get a de-risked, often clinical-stage asset at a lower entry price than building from discovery. In many recent deals the upfront plus equity has been a small fraction of the headline value, so milestone design and governance matter.

Process

  1. ScreenNon-confidential review of the asset: mechanism, stage, Chinese data, competitive landscape, IP and CMC status, and whether ex-China rights are free.
  2. Anonymous teaserA one-page blinded profile (modality, indication, stage, headline data already public) shared with selected investors and founders.
  3. NDA and deep diveInterested parties sign an NDA with the licensor; full data, regulatory history and CMC are reviewed with both teams.
  4. Term sheetTerritory, upfront (cash/equity), milestones, royalties, supply, governance and technology-transfer plan.
  5. Company formationNewCo incorporation, Series A close, management recruitment, license execution.
  6. Global developmentUS IND, bridging or global trials, and continued data sharing with the licensor's China program.

Confidentiality tiers

TIER 0

Public

Published papers, conference abstracts, company announcements. Used for screening and for this site.

Gate: none
TIER 1

Anonymous teaser

One page, licensor not named. Only information that is already public or approved by the owner for blinded circulation.

Gate: owner sign-off
TIER 2

NDA package

Licensor identified; non-public data summary, development plan, IP overview and indicative terms.

Gate: signed NDA with the asset owner
TIER 3

Data room

Full clinical, preclinical, CMC and regulatory documents, plus management Q&A, for serious bidders.

Gate: NDA + term-sheet stage, owner approval

What we need from each side

Asset owners

  • Rights available (territory, field) and any existing partners or options.
  • Stage and public data references; what can be shown blinded.
  • Your preferred structure: license, NewCo, co-development; equity expectations.
  • A decision-maker who can approve teaser circulation and NDAs.

Investors & founders

  • Search criteria: modality, indication, stage, geography of data.
  • Check size and whether you lead, co-lead or follow.
  • Whether a CEO or team is already in place.
  • Timeline and decision process.

Pharma BD

  • Therapeutic areas and modalities of interest; exclusions.
  • Preferred deal types: option, license, co-fund, later acquisition.
  • Stage gates you need to see before engaging.
  • A named contact for teaser review.

Submit a non-confidential asset teaser

Please do not send confidential information before an NDA is in place. Send only information that is already public or that you are willing to circulate without restriction. We will not forward anything that identifies your company without your written approval.

Email bd@inlightbio.com with the subject “Non-confidential asset teaser” and these fields:

  • Modality (small molecule, antibody, bispecific/TCE, ADC, siRNA, cell/gene therapy, other)
  • Target / mechanism (or “undisclosed”)
  • Lead indication(s)
  • Development stage and where trials ran
  • Headline data that is already public, with references
  • Rights available (e.g., ex-Greater China, global)
  • Patent status in one line (filed / granted; regions)
  • CMC / supply status in one line
  • Preferred deal type (NewCo, license, co-development, open)
  • Contact person and role
