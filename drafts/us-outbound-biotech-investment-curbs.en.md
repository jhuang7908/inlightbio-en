> Writing rule: keep the introduced bill and the Treasury notice of proposed rulemaking distinct. Do not describe either as an already-in-force ban, and do not present the NPRM as a mid-to-late November publication.

## At a glance

A check of the Congressional record for the week of about 16–23 November 2026 shows that H.R.9102, the Biotech Investment National Security Act (BINSA), remains where it was on 2 June 2026: introduced and referred to the House Committee on Financial Services. No new legislative action was found in this week’s window, so the bill should not be written as this week’s legislative news. The closer live process is the U.S. Treasury’s 26 October 2026 Federal Register notice of proposed rulemaking for the Outbound Investment Security Program (COINS). Written comments must be received by 7 December 2026. The proposal would add certain biotechnology activities to the set of prohibited technologies, but it is a proposed rule, not a final rule in force.

## Two tracks, kept separate

### 1. BINSA (H.R.9102)

- Full title: Biotech Investment National Security Act.
- Sponsor: Rep. John R. Moolenaar; introduced 2 June 2026 and referred to the House Committee on Financial Services.
- Congress.gov: https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/9102
- As of 23 November 2026, the latest action is still the 2 June referral. No new legislative action was found between 16 and 23 November 2026.

### 2. Treasury COINS proposed rule (closer to the current process, but dated before this week)

- Federal Register proposed rule, 26 October 2026: Outbound Investment Security Program Regulations (Docket TREAS-DO-2026-0595, RIN 1505-AC94).
  https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/26/2026-21786/outbound-investment-security-program-regulations
- Treasury press release: https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0667
- NPRM PDF: https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/206/COINS-NPRM.pdf
- Written comments must be received by **7 December 2026**.
- The proposal would bring certain biotechnology activities within the prohibited-technology scope (including using biological systems to modify, enhance, produce or control pharmaceutical compounds and their delivery systems, as well as some biomanufacturing equipment, sequencing and laboratory-automation tools). This is a notice of proposed rulemaking, not a final rule.
- The publication date is 26 October, outside this week’s window. A policy brief should state that timeline clearly and not present the NPRM as a mid-to-late November release.

## Writing notes

- Do not describe BINSA or the COINS NPRM as an already-effective investment ban.
- Law-firm commentary has suggested that “pure licensing without an equity investment” may fall outside the draft restrictions, whereas the BINSA text would bring licensing in. That is commentary, not a Treasury FAQ. If used, attribute it and check it against the rule text; do not state it as settled fact.
- Any effective date of a final rule must follow the officially published text. Comments that mention a statutory deadline around 13 March 2027 should be re-checked against Treasury or statutory text before they are used.
