NEWCO PARTNERING

From an under-partnered asset to a US NewCo

We source under-partnered clinical and preclinical assets globally, with deep coverage of China-origin programs, and assess whether each one fits a NewCo, a straight license or a co-development deal. For assets that fit, we help structure US NewCos: rights and territory, upfront, equity, milestones and royalties, and the development plan. We make BD and investor introductions to US venture investors, company-creation firms, management teams and pharma partners. Confidential material is shared in tiers, starting with an anonymous teaser and moving to an NDA package only with the owner's approval.

How NewCo partnering worksEmail bd@inlightbio.com

Sponsorship

Sponsorship supports literature access, illustration tools and email operations, so the science and deal briefs can stay free. Two options: a named sponsor, shown with name and link on the home page and in the weekly email footer; or sponsored content, clearly labelled “Supported by …” and laid out separately from editorial content. Sponsors have no say over topic selection or conclusions. Full policy on the Sponsorship page; inquiries to contact@inlightbio.com.

Engagement

Engagement terms are agreed case by case; contact bd@inlightbio.com for NewCo matters or contact@inlightbio.com for reports.

InLight Bio's editorial coverage is independent of NewCo mandates. If a company covered on this site is a client or counterparty, we disclose it at the top of the item.
