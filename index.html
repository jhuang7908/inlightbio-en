An LLM platform lets epilepsy patients text with their EEG device; in a simulation, their replies cut false seizure alarms by about 80%

In 13 inpatients at Penn's epilepsy monitoring unit, a chatbot linked to live scalp or intracranial EEG answered questions, sent seizure and sleep alerts and collected feedback; in a pseudoprospective simulation, retraining the detector on that feedback alone cut false alarms by 77–81% without losing sensitivity. Live sessions used only the baseline detector.

    In vivo LNP delivery generates CAR-NK cells

    Nature Biotechnology · Oct 13, 2026 · doi:10.1038/s41587-026-03310-1 Correspondence: Daniel G. Anderson (MIT)

    The authors built NK cell-targeted lipid nanoparticles using engineered biparatopic NK cell ligands for receptor-specific uptake and mRNA delivery, reaching up to about 80% NK cell transfection in mice with minimal off-target effects. Systemic delivery of chimeric antigen receptor mRNA generated CAR-NK cells in vivo: CD19-targeted CAR-NKs completely depleted circulating B cells in healthy mice, and CD3-targeted CAR-NKs eliminated nearly all autoreactive T cells in an autoimmune hepatitis model. In solid tumors, nanoparticles targeting the inhibitory checkpoint NKG2A enhanced antitumor efficacy by relieving Qa-1b-mediated immunosuppression, and optimized particles enabled regression of two humanized hematologic malignancy models.

    D2C7 antibody-toxin conjugate: a phase 1 trial in recurrent glioma

    Nature Medicine · Oct 16, 2026 · doi:10.1038/s41591-026-04715-w Correspondence: Vidyalakshmi Chandramohan (Duke University Medical Center)

    D2C7-ATC is a recombinant antibody-toxin conjugate targeting wild-type EGFR and EGFRvIII. In a single-arm, single-site, open-label phase 1 trial (NCT02303678), 81 patients with biopsy-confirmed recurrent malignant glioma received one infusion by convection-enhanced delivery. Median overall survival was 7.9 months among the 72 patients with IDH wild-type recurrent glioblastoma; the recommended phase 2 dose was 6,920 ng/ml, and the maximum tolerated dose was exceeded at 35,032 ng/ml where four patients had dose-limiting toxicities. Treatment-related adverse events were mainly grade 1-2 (67%); eight patients died on trial, all unrelated to D2C7-ATC. In univariate analyses, patients with IDH wild-type recurrent glioblastoma whose pre-treatment tumour D2C7 antibody reactivity score was below 1.5+ had a median overall survival of 17.38 months.

    Why TCR antigen specificity prediction fails to generalise: a preprint perspective

    bioRxiv · Oct 18, 2026 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.12.758456 Correspondence: Hashem Koohy (University of Oxford)

    This preprint perspective asks why increasingly sophisticated models of TCR antigen specificity, from small neural networks to deep generative models and large protein language models, largely fail in independent benchmarks to generalise to TCRs or pMHCs absent from development data. Analysing the largest public TCR:pMHC repositories, the authors report significant biases towards a few antigens and MHC molecules, sparse sampling of TCR sequence space per pMHC, and inconsistent or inaccurate data collation. They argue evaluations must measure antigen-specific discrimination rather than confounders, and that metrics should match the downstream application.
