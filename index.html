Anti-GDF-15 visugromab plus nivolumab before surgery in bladder cancer: pCR 33% vs 8%

The phase 2a GDFATHER-NEO trial enrolled 31 patients with muscle-invasive bladder cancer who were ineligible for or declined cisplatin. Three neoadjuvant cycles of nivolumab plus the anti-GDF-15 antibody visugromab, versus nivolumab plus placebo, gave local pCR 33% vs 8%, MPR 60% vs 23%, radiographic ORR 60% vs 15% and centrally reviewed pCR 47% vs 17%; grade 3–4 TRAEs 13% vs 20%. Response tracked with baseline proliferating CD8⁺ T cell density, not with PD-L1 or GDF-15 levels.

    Bispecific T-cell agonist invika shows early activity in ICB-resistant solid tumours

    Nature Medicine · Oct 23, 2026 · doi:10.1038/s41591-026-04711-0 Correspondence: Ke Liu (Marengo Therapeutics), Lillian L. Siu (Princess Margaret Cancer Centre), James L. Gulley (NCI)

    invikafusp alfa (invika) is a first-in-class bispecific dual T-cell-agonist antibody that selectively targets the Vβ6/Vβ10 T-cell subsets. START-001 is a multicenter phase 1/2 single-agent trial (NCT05592626) enrolling mostly patients with antigen-rich solid tumours resistant to immune checkpoint blockade (ICB). As of 12 March 2025, phase 1 dose escalation had enrolled 34 patients across doses of 0.01 to 0.16 mg/kg, with the maximum tolerated dose not reached; the initial phase 2 stage enrolled 18 patients. On safety, 2 patients had reversible dose-limiting toxicities. The most common treatment-related adverse event was cytokine release syndrome, mainly grade 1 and 2. No treatment-related deaths and no non-laboratory grade 4 adverse events were reported. On efficacy, the objective response rate at the optimal biological doses (0.08 and 0.12 mg/kg) was 14.8% (4/27). Confirmed partial responses were seen in cold tumours such as microsatellite-stable colorectal cancer and PD-L1-negative non-small-cell lung cancer; no responses occurred below 0.08 mg/kg. The recommended phase 2 dose was set at 0.08 mg/kg. Significance: the study offers a new approach of activating T cells by TCR Vβ subset in ICB-resistant cold tumours, and sets dose by pharmacodynamic data rather than the maximum tolerated dose. Note: this is an early single-arm study with an efficacy sample of only 27 patients, awaiting validation by the ongoing phase 2.
