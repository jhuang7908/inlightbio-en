THIS WEEK'S LEAD · SCIENCE

Multicenter series of cilta-cel enterocolitis: 3.0% cumulative incidence, 41% died with active disease

The US Multiple Myeloma Immunotherapy Consortium retrospectively adjudicated 54 suspected cases of cilta-cel immune effector cell-associated enterocolitis (IEC-EC) from 8 institutions: 41 were IEC-EC. Through September 2025, the cumulative incidence among 1,308 cilta-cel recipients was 3.0% (39 of 1,308; 95% CI 2.1–4.1%). Median onset was 75 days; 41% died with active disease. Treatment subgroups were small and nonrandomized and should not be read as an efficacy ranking.

THIS WEEK'S LEAD · DEALS & FINANCING

Pfizer in-licenses three preclinical in vivo CAR-T programs from Starna, with $330 million upfront

On November 18, 2026, Pfizer announced an exclusive license with Starna Therapeutics. Pfizer obtains exclusive rights outside Greater China to develop, manufacture and commercialize three pre-IND in vivo CAR-T programs for autoimmune disease, with a $330 million upfront payment, up to $2.3 billion in development and commercial milestones, and royalties on net sales of approved products. Pfizer also receives rights to use Starna’s in vivo CAR-T technology in inflammation and immunology and in oncology, and an option to expand the territory to Greater China. Pfizer will lead clinical development outside Greater China and plans to manufacture finished drug product for the initial clinical trials at its U.S. facilities.
