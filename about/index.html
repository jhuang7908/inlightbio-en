About InLight Bio

InLight Bio is the English edition of 前沿追踪 (Frontier Digest, inlight.therasik.com), published by Qiyuan Zhiyan (启元智研, Beijing).

What we cover

China biopharma going global: out-licensing deals, NewCo formation, acquisitions of China-originated assets, and the clinical data that drives their value. Readers are US investors, pharma business-development teams and NewCo founders who need a fast, sourced view of what is happening in China.

Each brief gives the headline terms with upfront and milestone payments separated, a short summary of the science and the deal logic, and a link to the original announcement or trade-press source.

Editorial principles

  • Sources. Every item links to its original source. Items based only on a press release or secondary coverage say so.
  • Deal amounts. Upfront and milestones are listed separately, as publicly disclosed. Nothing here is investment advice.
  • Translation. English items are translated from the Chinese edition without adding facts or numbers.
  • Illustrations. Images on deal items are AI-generated decorative illustrations, not data or journal figures.
  • Conflicts. We also run a NewCo services business. If a company we cover is a client or counterparty, we disclose it at the top of the item. Sponsored content, if any, is labelled and kept separate from editorial content.
  • Corrections. Send corrections to contact@inlightbio.com; we mark corrections on the item.

Publisher

Qiyuan Zhiyan (启元智研) is a Beijing-based company providing scientific content and biopharma business services. It publishes and maintains both editions of the site.
